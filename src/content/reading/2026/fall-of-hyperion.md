After listening to my gripes with the anticlimax of _Hyperion_, I heard from a few people
that _The Fall of Hyperion_ should really be considered part of the same volume. The
story's climax couldn't fit into _Hyperion_'s _Canterbury Tales_ format and ended up
getting pushed to the next installment. I think that's certainly right. Inside a more
traditional narrative structure, _The Fall of Hyperion_ wraps up storylines from book 1
and expands on the backstory of the universe.

While the book was engaging and entertaining I wouldn't say I felt particularly satisfied
by the end. Simmons continues to rely on heavy exposition to resolve plot tension, and
this time I actually had trouble following along and keeping track of the revealed
information. Many characters spouted their own philosophy, and at least for me it didn't
really land. The main emotional arc ended up intertwined with the main twist, and both
fell flat for me, in addition to having some pretty disturbing implications for some
previous storylines.
